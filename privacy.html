3DPlop! – Privacy policy

3DPlop! by CriC. Last update: October 2026.

3DPlop! has no account and does not collect, store or send any personal data on a server.

  • Camera – used only in augmented reality, to show the pool on a real surface. The images are processed on the phone and never recorded or sent, except in the videos you choose to make. AR runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), provided by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Videos – made and stored on your phone. They leave it only when you share them (with the app you pick) or save them to your gallery.
  • Settings – stored on your phone only.
  • Ads (free version, when enabled) – Google AdMob shows ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first (Google's consent form); with it, AdMob may use your device's advertising ID. You can change your choice at any time in the app (Settings › Privacy › Ad choices). See how Google uses data.
  • Purchases – handled by Google Play; the app only learns whether premium is unlocked.
  • Children – the app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Contact: the developer e-mail shown on the store listing.
